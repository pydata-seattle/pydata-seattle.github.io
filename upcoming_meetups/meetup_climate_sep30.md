# PyData Seattle: AI for Climate and Earth

**Date:** Wednesday, September 30, 2025  
**Time:** 5:00 PM PDT  
**Location:** PLUS84, 2401 3rd Ave Unit 1210, Seattle, WA 98121 (Hybrid)  
**RSVP:** [meetup.com/pydata_seattle](https://www.meetup.com/pydata_seattle/events/315789388/)

---

An evening exploring how AI, open-source software, and public data can help address real climate and environmental challenges. The event features practical talks, live demos, and community projects.

**Topics include:**
- Satellite imagery and geospatial AI
- Open models for Earth and climate data
- Wildfire, flood, weather, and water forecasting
- Air-quality and energy-system intelligence
- Climate-risk analysis and adaptation
- AI agents combining scientific, sensor, satellite, and policy data
- Open-source tools and public datasets for climate action
- Climate-tech startup and research demos

We welcome software engineers, data scientists, researchers, climate professionals, students, founders, and open-source contributors.
