# Code of Conduct

PyData Seattle follows the [NumFOCUS Code of Conduct](https://numfocus.org/code-of-conduct).

## Short Version

PyData Seattle is dedicated to providing a harassment-free community experience for everyone, regardless of gender, sexual orientation, gender identity and expression, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, or religion. We do not tolerate harassment of community members in any form.

## Reporting

To discuss any issues or concerns relating to the code of conduct or the behavior of anyone at a PyData Seattle event, please contact NumFOCUS Executive Director Leah Silen:

- Phone: +1 512-222-5449
- Email: <leah@numfocus.org>

You can also reach the PyData Seattle organising team at <seattle@pydata.org>.

## Full Version

The full NumFOCUS Code of Conduct is available at [numfocus.org/code-of-conduct](https://numfocus.org/code-of-conduct).
